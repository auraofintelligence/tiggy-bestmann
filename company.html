Love, desire and company

A glance across a party, an invitation and the delight of wanting more time together.

Tiggy likes the moment a conversation becomes a flirtation. There is laughter, a little uncertainty and the growing pleasure of realising the interest goes both ways. He wants affection and sex, companionship and adventure, with women who enjoy exploring that life with him.

An evening in Mexico City

On a rooftop surrounded by art and conversation, two women invite him into their company. One is pregnant. Someone’s story sets all three laughing; a question about his music keeps them talking. The attraction grows within an evening that already has plenty happening.

A first encounter begins with the woman or women approaching him. Tiggy answers with his own humour, interest and desire. Each has something to bring to the meeting, and each enjoys finding out what the others have in mind.

Desire in many forms

His attraction reaches across different bodies, backgrounds and personalities. A woman’s cheekiness, energy or delight in an idea is as much a part of the encounter as the way she looks at him. Quiet warmth and exuberant flirtation give him different pleasures.

His desires include conceiving a child or children with consenting women who share that wish, and appreciating the beauty of pregnancy. He hopes to meet single women who are interested in him, start the conversation and invite him into an honest, uncomplicated connection.

He respects existing relationships and vows, with no wish to draw anyone away from a partner or husband. Affection, sex and adventure follow mutual interest, with intentions openly expressed. Conception belongs to a particular shared wish, rather than a goal for every encounter.

Love across a travelling life

He enjoys the freshness of a new meeting and the familiarity of someone he already loves. A journey brings departures as well as arrivals. He wants relationships that stay alive through messages, shared projects and occasions to be together again, alongside the freedom to keep exploring.

Luke’s travels in Thailand included both a journey with a girlfriend and later visits alone. His memories of forthright invitations, friendship and attraction give Tiggy’s travel romances a lived starting point. The fiction follows its own encounters from there.

Several people, larger possibilities

Global Group Marriages extends the horizon towards relationships involving several partners across countries, with affection, shared pleasure, parenting and practical life entwined. The United Nations of Love imagines the UN as one loving, sexually alive, cross-cultural marriage raising children together. GAJRA Earth places that thought experiment within a wider planetary life.

For Tiggy, those possibilities grow from the desire to love and be loved in abundance. Some stories follow a brief meeting; others follow a reunion, a circle of lovers or a shared future. He remains curious about the people involved and the life they want to enjoy together.
