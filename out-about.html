World travel

Every country and territory, with work, romance and big experiences along the way.

Tiggy’s ambition is every country and territory. He wants to meet, share, learn and teach, with enough happening along the way to make the journey worth remembering. There is work involved, time is short and a good invitation is worth paying attention to.

Bangkok, before the boat arrives

Beside a canal, two women start talking to him while a passenger boat approaches. Their conversation runs from the gathering they are heading to, to the creative work that brought him into town. The engine grows louder. They are still laughing when it is time to board.

A city has its own pace, sounds and everyday pleasures. Tiggy enjoys being amongst them with people who have their own reasons for being there. Romance, work and sightseeing cross paths during an ordinary journey through the city.

The working traveller

Luke’s UK working holiday took him through bar work, office demolition at Tower 42 and a gift-card factory before the festival season. Darwin later brought airport check-in, boarding and airside work on the Cairns-Darwin-Singapore route. Bowen Basin shutdowns brought another working landscape entirely.

Those memories give Tiggy experience of travel from several directions: arriving for a job, helping other people depart, building a temporary event and finishing work within a tight window. His practical skills travel alongside his appetite for company and fun.

Different places, different pleasures

India, Nepal, Thailand and Abu Dhabi belong to Luke’s travel history. Tiggy’s wish list reaches further: outback gatherings and beach cultures, sport, city nightlife, high society and festivals across continents. He enjoys the variety, from a casual game with new friends to an evening where everybody has dressed splendidly.

A short working visit still holds encounters worth following. Global Founder Atlas connects the wider work with research, residencies and projects. Right Place, Right Time follows the relationship between useful work and the next opportunity.

The oracle follows the opportunity

His travel oracle brings together invitations, work, events, resources, seasons and the interests he feels like pursuing. A festival date, a promising swell or a message from someone he wants to see gives the next decision its shape.

The ambition spans the globe; the route develops through life. He wants time for the experiences that matter, including a return journey when the company is worth it. Music, romance and a coast with good waves each give him a different reason to go.
